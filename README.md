# IMedora

IMedora is a smart medical device management platform designed to help healthcare facilities manage, monitor, and maintain their medical equipment efficiently.

## Project Status

🚧 Currently under development.

## Team

* Development Team
* Hardware / Biomedical Engineering Team
* AI Team
* UI/UX Team

## Main Goals

* Medical device management
* Maintenance tracking
* Preventive and corrective maintenance
* Maintenance requests and tickets
* Device information and history
* AI-powered assistance
* Reports and analytics
